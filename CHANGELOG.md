# Changelog

## 2026.11.4

- Punctuation added by speech engines at the end of what you say is ignored, so places, contacts and app names no longer get a trailing period
- Switching between the small and large speech model no longer downloads it again
- Setting to delete the speech model you're not using
- Black theme: the skills list has no outline and sits right below the top bar

## 2026.11.3

- Black launch screen with the white Assistio logo
- Setting for a large English speech model (128 MB) that makes fewer recognition mistakes than the small one

## 2026.11.2

- New app icon and sidebar logo (black in light mode, white in dark mode)
- Dark theme uses neutral greys instead of slate blue
- Black theme makes cards black too, with a thin outline
- Conversation shows your messages as bubbles on the right and replies on the left in normal-size text
- Skills list heading is now "Active Skills:"
- New example phrases for skills
- Sidebar says "Can I help you?"

## 2026.11.1

- Text messages: "text Mom saying I'm on my way" or just "text Mom". Assistio reads the message back and sends it only after you say yes
- Reminders in Tasks.org: "remind me to call Mom at noon". Creates a task with a due time and a reminder
- Tasks in Tasks.org: "add buy milk to my tasks"
- Renamed to Assistio (ass.is.tio) so it installs alongside Dicio
